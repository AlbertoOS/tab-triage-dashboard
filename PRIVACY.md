# Privacy policy for Tab Triage Dashboard

Tab Triage Dashboard helps you review and manage open browser tabs. It does not require an account, run an application backend, or include analytics or advertising trackers. It does not upload your tab list to the developer.

## Information used by the extension

The extension reads open-tab metadata, including titles, URLs, favicons, window identifiers and tab status. It uses this information in your browser to display the dashboard, search, sort, filter, identify duplicates and carry out the tab actions you request. It does not read the contents of web pages or record your keystrokes or mouse movements.

The restore action asks your browser to reopen its most recently closed tab or window. The browser manages that session history; the extension does not maintain a separate archive of closed tabs. Restoration is browser-wide, not an exact undo of a dashboard action or batch.

## Local storage and retention

Ignored URLs, ignored domains and the favicon size preference are saved in the extension's local browser storage until you change or remove them, clear that storage, or uninstall the extension. The extension does not use browser storage sync to send these settings between devices. The live tab list is held in dashboard memory rather than saved as a persistent browsing-history database.

You can review and remove ignored entries in the dashboard. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. Browser-managed tabs, history and recently closed sessions remain subject to your browser's own controls.

## Site icons and network requests

The dashboard displays favicon URLs supplied by your browser. Loading these images can cause network requests to the sites or icon hosts involved, which may receive your IP address and normal browser request information. Missing or failed icons are replaced with a bundled image. The extension does not send its tab list to an analytics service or developer-operated server.

## Exported links

Link exports are plain-text files created on your device at your request. They contain browsing URLs, which may include sensitive information. Review them before sharing. Exported files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them; uninstalling the extension does not delete those files.

## Data use and sharing

Tab information is used only to provide the extension's tab-management features. The developer does not sell it, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or receive it through an extension backend. The extension does not upload link exports. User-directed sharing of an exported file and site-icon requests are separate from developer data collection.

Use of browser data is limited to the user-facing functionality described here, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the project's [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/AlbertoOS/tab-triage-dashboard/issues). Issues are public: do not include private tab lists, sensitive URLs or credentials.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page. Future changes to the extension's data handling will be reflected in this policy and applicable store disclosures.
